Greedy evaluation counts are total unique score evaluations, including partial construction states. Annealing first-hit counts measure when an optimum is first found among successful runs. Probabilities printed in tables are rounded; machine-readable files retain fuller precision. The document also gives methods, parameters and the complete hardware task inventory.
